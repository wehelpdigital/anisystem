Reel music lives here.

Drop audio files (.mp3, .m4a, .aac, .ogg, .wav) into this folder and they
appear in the reel editor's music picker automatically — no code change, no
database row. The track's name becomes its title, so "morning-harvest.mp3"
shows as "Morning Harvest".

Nothing is bundled on purpose. Music published under a farm's name has to be
music that farm is allowed to publish, and that is a licensing decision rather
than a coding one. Sources that are free to use commercially with no
attribution include Pixabay Music and the CC0 corner of the Free Music
Archive; anything CC-BY works too as long as the credit is shown.

A farmer can always pick a sound from their own phone instead — that path
needs nothing in here.
