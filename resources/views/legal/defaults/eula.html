4. Why Advice can still be wrong

Anee's Advice comes from a careful, scientific approach: years of weather records for your place, forecasts, the climate outlook, soil and crop knowledge, registered products and what you report. That approach gives you a better chance than guessing, but farming is full of things no one can know in advance, including:

  • the weather on your own field (micro weather and microclimate), sudden storms, floods, drought, heat and cold, and forecasts that change;
  • pests, diseases and weeds that arrive or spread unexpectedly;
  • differences in soil and water within the same lot;
  • the quality of seeds, fertilizers and chemicals, including fake, old or wrongly stored products;
  • how and when work is actually done, by whom, and with what equipment;
  • market prices, demand, buyers and transport;
  • information you gave that is missing, outdated or wrong;
  • errors in outside data, such as forecasts, maps or price records, and breaks in the internet or in the App.

Because of this, Advice can be incomplete, late or wrong. We do not promise any harvest, yield, quality, price, income or saving.
