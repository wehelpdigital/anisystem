The short version. Many pictures on anee.io were taken by other people, who share them under free licences. The line under a picture says who took it and which licence it has. To reuse one, follow that licence. Pictures with no name under them are not free to copy, so please ask us first.

This page explains who owns the pictures on our public pages and how you may use them. It covers pictures only. The rest of anee.io is covered by our Terms of Service.

1. Pictures with a name and a licence under them

The line under the picture names the photographer, the licence and where the picture came from, for example "Photo: Juan Dela Cruz, CC BY 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons". The picture still belongs to the person named. We use it under that licence, and you may too if you follow it:

  • CC0 or Public domain: free to use for anything. No credit is needed, though it is kind to give one.
  • CC BY: free to use, including for business, as long as you name the photographer, link to the licence and say if you changed the picture.
  • CC BY SA: the same as CC BY, and anything you make from the picture must be shared under the same licence.
  • Unsplash License: free to use, but you may not sell the picture as it is.

The full wording of the Creative Commons licences is on the Creative Commons website.

2. Pictures from news reports

Our farm news roundups show a picture from the original report, marked with the name of the news site and a link to that report. Those pictures belong to the news site. To reuse one, please ask them.

3. Pictures with no name under them

Screens of the app, and photos with no name under them, belong to us or to those who license them to us. Please do not copy or reuse them without our written permission.

4. How to ask, or tell us about a mistake

Write to support@anee.io and tell us which picture you mean and how you want to use it. If you took one of the pictures here and the credit is wrong, or you would like it taken down, write to the same address and we will fix it.
