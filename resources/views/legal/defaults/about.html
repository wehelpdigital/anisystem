Made by farmers, for farmers. anee.io is a farm-management app from the Philippines. It helps growers plan the season, keep good records and make better decisions, with a friendly AI technician named Anee at their side.

What anee.io is

anee.io brings the whole cropping season into one place, right on your phone. Plan your seasons and lots, and set the activities for every stage of the crop. Keep track of workers and pay, materials and inventory, costs and harvests, and watch it all come together in clear reports. Draw your farm on a map, keep notes, photos and voice memos, check the weather for your lots, and share the day's work with your team.

It runs in any web browser, and you can install it on your phone or computer like an app. With Offline Mode, the pages you need stay ready in the field, even where the signal is weak.

Who it is for

anee.io was built for Filipino farmers, whether they grow rice, corn, vegetables, fruit trees or more, and for the way they really work:

  • Small farmers who want a simple, reliable record of every season.
  • Farm owners and managers running several lots and seasons with a team of workers.
  • Technicians, cooperatives and agripreneurs who plan, monitor and advise on farms.
  • New farmers learning the craft who want a guide beside them.

Meet Anee, your AI technician

Anee is anee.io's farm technician: an AI assistant who knows your season. Ask her about a yellowing leaf, a pest you spotted, when to fertilise or what to plant next, and she answers in plain, friendly words. She can look at the photos you send, read the season you are asking about, and run deeper analyses such as When to Plant, What to Plant, Variety Research and Crop Protocol.

Anee runs on credits, and her advice is a guide: a knowledgeable second opinion, never a replacement for your own judgement, the product label, or your local agricultural technician.

A community of growers

Farming is better together. In the anee.io Community, farmers share photos and stories from the field, ask questions, join discussions, and learn from one another and from our team. Kindness is the rule.

Our mission

To help every farmer reach the best yield and income their land can give, by putting practical planning, good records and expert guidance in their hands, at a price a small farm can afford.

Weather, pests and prices will always be uncertain. We believe the farms that do best are the ones that notice early and act in time, and that a farm is a business that deserves the same good tools every other business already has.
